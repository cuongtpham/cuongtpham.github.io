## Overview

This research applies deep learning and computer vision to structural damage assessment from imagery. It includes post-tornado building damage recognition and bridge component classification and damage detection from UAV images.

## Research Contributions

- Collaboration on object detection, damage severity classification, and model performance evaluation for post-tornado buildings.
- Collection and annotation of UAV bridge imagery and development of convolutional neural network models for bridge damage detection.
