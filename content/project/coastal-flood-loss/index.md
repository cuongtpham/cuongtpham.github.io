## Overview

This research assesses coastal flood damage and economic loss under storm surge and sea-level rise scenarios. It connects AI-derived first-floor elevations with community-scale building inventories and the IN-CORE platform.

## Research Focus

The work combines building elevation information with coastal flood risk assessment to support analysis of infrastructure vulnerability.
